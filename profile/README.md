## 🏢 Who we are

**LQMS** is a consulting firm specializing in **contact center and BPO operational excellence**.
We work alongside BPO leadership teams as their quality, workforce, and continuous improvement partner. We turn operational data into measurable performance and help them become certification-ready.

> *Quality is not inspected in, it is built in, across every interaction, every queue, every interval.*

## 🧭 Our methodology: PEICC™

**PEICC™** is LQMS's proprietary framework for contact center operational excellence. It brings together quality, workforce, and process management in one auditable system, supported by a formal manual and an audit workbook.
